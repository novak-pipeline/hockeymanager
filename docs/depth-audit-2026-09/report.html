The game looks deeper than it plays

Five auditors read the code system by system and compared each one with modern Football Manager, and one of them played a full year to measure the loop. The finding is consistent: presentation is ahead of substance. Most systems have the screen, the words and the voice, but thin state behind them. So consequences fade, the world doesn't push back, and the game rarely tells you whether you were right.

02 · Why it keeps feeling like placeholders

Eight root causes

These are why "consequences matter" turned into a popup, and why each fix so far landed at about 60%. Fix these and many individual complaints go away together.

1

People have one number for memory, and it forgets in two weeks

Every conversation, promise, scratch and trade rumour ends as morale += n, and morale drifts back to neutral about 5% a day. Whatever you did fades before it can matter. The narrative design doc explicitly banned new mechanics, so depth was capped at delivery.

condition.ts:176, 254 · NARRATIVE-ENGINE.md non-goals

2

Write-only state

Plenty is recorded that nothing reads: room morale (for the sim), respect in the room, pundit rapport, owner commitments, "surplus" status, social groups, signing bonuses, revenue. "Morale contagion" exists only as a comment. The screens describe systems the engine doesn't run.

coverageTilt() has zero callers · dynamics.ts:15 · ownerMeddling.ts:133

3

You play under fog; the AI doesn't

AI clubs draft from true potential. The trade screen shows the AI's real yes/no before you send. The "rival clubs circling" in free agency come from a hash. Market size comes from a hash of the team id. Your scouting is real work while everyone else cheats, which is both a fairness problem and a cheese problem.

offseason.ts:873 · career.ts:13135, 12181

4

Only your club is simulated as people

Concerns, promises, happiness and line synergy exist only for your club. AI GM personas have seven traits, but only aggression changes anything, and only in offers to you. The world has results but not personalities.

career.ts:3647, 2939 · trades.ts persona reads

5

The economy stops binding

The cap grows 4.5% a year while salary asks stay in fixed dollars, and the floor isn't enforced. By about 2032 nobody feels cap pressure: no second-contract squeeze, no depth leaving for money. That's the likeliest reason a bot built a 17-season juggernaut.

contracts.ts:51, 169 · cap reaches about $286M by 2049

6

Nothing ever tells you whether you were right

No development history is stored, scouts are never graded against outcomes, and staff advice has no receipts. The development focus "biases growth" and then goes silent. Without feedback, depth reads as decoration.

EXCELLENCE gap #6 · no advice or credibility ledger found

7

Watching and simming are different games

Most tactics only exist in the full engine used for watched games. The quick sim behind Continue ignores them, so the playbook barely matters, and watching versus simming quietly changes your expected results. Watched games are also fully decided before playback, so you can't influence them.

quickSim.ts:674 · career.ts:6941

8

Tests built on values the game never produces

Personality tests use a 0–100 scale while players are generated 1–20, and mid-season code reads stats that only exist after rollover. Both bug classes hit the promise system, the centrepiece of the consequence design, and the tests stayed green.

livingLedger.test.ts:120 · career.ts:3765

03 · Fix regardless of plan

Confirmed bugs

Found by reading the code, with file references in the raw audits. Each is small. Red means it breaks trust or fairness.

Scene and negotiation ice-time promises always "kept"They have no due date and are auto-kept at rollover. 19 authored options are affected.

Ignoring concerns is free and silences the roomOpen concerns never expire, and two unanswered ones block any new ones.

Medical risk shows fatigue the model ignoresThe head physio changes nothing.
Squad status has no capTag everyone "key player" for free weekly morale.
Calendar and label packThe takeover summer is dated a year early. Continue says "review" but opens the draft. "Camp is over" mail arrives on day one. There's a 60-item dump in June.

04 · The plan

Fix the foundations, then build your epics on them

Ordered so each phase makes the next one real. Your own requests from this session are marked. Every phase is judged by one rule: fun outweighs realism, and nothing interrupts you unless it's a real decision or a payoff.

PHASE 0

Trust and quiet

About a week. Stops the game working against you.
The bug list aboveSEvery item, each with a regression test that uses values the game actually generates.
Interruption dietyou askedContinue always just advances; no more "Continue — staff meeting" signposts. Trade offers interrupt only above a real threshold, the rest go to a weekly digest. The overlay holds only for results, decisions and big stories (83% down to about 35%). Staff meetings fire on events, with a frequency setting. Routine board meetings are cut to three moments a year. Target: about 4.1 clicks per game down to about 2.2.
Text-to-speech and the AI writer off by defaultyou flaggedBoth kept behind a hidden flag, not deleted. The phone becomes a visual device, with the decision on the handset itself. Prose comes from authored, context-varied lines built on the richer Phase 1 facts.
PHASE 1

Foundations that make depth real

The state the rest of the plan stands on.
Economy indexed to the cap, plus a parity harnessS → MSalary asks scale with the cap, the floor is enforced for AI clubs, and every season's league table and champion is recorded. Targets: points spread SD 12–16, 6 or more distinct champions per decade, no 17-year juggernauts.
Happiness ledger and GM trustLMorale becomes a sum of caused, visible, lasting factors, with an FM-style happiness panel where each factor links to what you did. Tone lands differently by personality, trust and history. Concerns escalate on a clock: agent, press leak, social group, leaders' meeting, trade request.
Hindsight everywhereyou likedA development ledger (growth by cause), scout track records graded at +2 and +4 seasons, and staff-advice receipts with credibility. This is the "did it work?" layer, and it feeds Season Wrapped.
Continue honours tacticsMPort the seven proven tactics into the quick sim and calibrate them against the full sim. Then the GM sets a game plan the coach carries out, and the Watch-versus-Play gap closes.

05 · Decisions

What's settled and what needs your call

Settled
Fun outweighs realism

Realism serves the fun. Penalties only for exploit patterns, always telegraphed, forgiving, never a lockout.

Settled
No "click here for event"

Continue always advances. Real moments open on their own; everything else goes to the briefing or inbox.

Settled
The board at three moments

Preseason expectations, the deadline checkpoint, the season verdict. Its mood is a line of prose in between.

Your call
Voice off by default for 1.0?

Recommended. It runs slower than real time on your machine and can't keep voices consistent. The code stays behind a flag, and pre-rendered lines for tentpole moments are possible later.

Your call
AI writer off by default too?

Recommended. Given thin facts it writes lines like "I'm glad the GM heard me", and authored, context-varied lines do better at no cost. If the model returns later, it should be to understand what you type to characters, not to decorate cards.

Your call
Lift the "no new mechanics" rule

Phase 1 adds real state (a happiness ledger, trust, a development ledger). That breaks the narrative doc's non-goal, and the new state is what the depth needs.

Your call
Bring main up to date

main is 140 commits behind improve-loop, which gave one agent a stale copy. I'd fast-forward it after today's merges are green.
