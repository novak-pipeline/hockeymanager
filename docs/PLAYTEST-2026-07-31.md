# Playtest notes — 2026-07-31 (user, Pittsburgh save)

Second full regroup. The nav ship-blocker is fixed, so this playtest reached
screens the last one couldn't. The findings cluster hard around **two systems
that are shallow where the game most needs depth**: the trade market and the
re-signing window. Everything else is polish, correctness or presentation.

Grouped by system, ranked within each. `bar` ties to docs/EXCELLENCE.md.

## A. THE TRADE SYSTEM — the headline epic

The user's words: *"trades are way too easy to make… we need DEEP realism and
intricacy to our value and trading system."* Treat A1–A3 as one epic; A4–A6 are
bugs that fall out of the same area.

| # | Finding | Notes | Bar |
|---|---------|-------|-----|
| A1 | **Trade value shown as a raw number** (`22.9`, `29.7`, `60.7`) | Wanted: a bar/meter like the EA NHL games, or another non-numeric read. A bare decimal reads like a spreadsheet and implies false precision. | B7.3 |
| A2 | **Value is global, not per-club** | *"each team should value players differently depending on their views of the player / their needs."* A rebuilder and a contender must not price the same 33-year-old alike; a club thin at RD must pay more for RD. This is the real fix behind A3 — one true value is what makes trades feel mechanical. | B3.2 |
| A3 | **Trades are far too easy** | Concrete: a 22-year-old at 4★ current / 5★ potential offered straight up for Bryan Rust (28, $5.13M×3). No AI club makes that trade. Needs real reluctance: retained salary, term, age curves, positional scarcity, roster fit, and a GM who says no. | B3.2 |
| A4 | **Only NHL-roster players are tradeable** | AHL players, prospects and rights-held juniors can't be moved. Picks too (verify). Guts the asset market. | B3.2 |
| A5 | **Trading Erik Karlsson produced no news article** | A franchise defenceman changes teams in silence. Should be a headline, a feed reaction, and a chronicle entry. | B4.5 |
| A6 | **Trade offers do not block Continue** | An open offer must gate the sim and be flagged in the topbar, the way other beats now name themselves (Gap #1). Right now you can sim straight past a standing offer. | B2.2 |
| A7 | **Can sim past the trade deadline** | The deadline is the season's biggest decision point and it slides by unattended. Must be a hard beat gate. | B3.2 |

## B. THE RE-SIGNING WINDOW — the second epic  ✅ BUILT

*"Thats a big one to handle."* Currently one screen with instant accepts.

| # | Finding | Bar |
|---|---------|-----|
| B1 | **Re-signing is a single screen with instant accepts** — should be the real window, playing out **over time**: offers are considered, not answered on the spot. | B3.2 |
| B2 | **No qualifying offers for our own RFAs** | B3.2 |
| B3 | **Offer sheets not handled in the window** (the async engine exists — #168/#183 — but the window doesn't surface it) | B3.2 |
| B4 | **Too easy to sign a player** by offering the same AAV he asked for but more years. Term has to cost something. | B3.2 |

**Shipped 2026-07-31.** The re-sign stage is now a 4-day June window
(`OffseasonState.resignDay`), each Continue another day before July 1.

- **B1** — `submitResignOffer` tables terms into an async queue
  (`Career.resignOffers`, the same shape as `faPendingOffers` / `pendingOfferSheets`
  / `pendingTrades`); the camp answers a day or two later with **accept**, a
  **priced counter** you can sign as written (`acceptResignCounter`), a
  **refusal** that burns patience, or a **walk-out** when the patience is gone.
  Decision logic lives in the pure `src/engine/league/resignWindow.ts`.
- **B2** — qualifying offers on the real CBA ladder (`qualifyingOffer`, 110/105/100%
  of prior salary). Obvious keepers are auto-tendered; the bubble is a real
  choice. Tendering keeps his rights (and exposes him to offer sheets and
  arbitration); walking away makes him unrestricted for nothing. A depth RFA can
  simply *take* the QO as a one-year deal.
- **B3** — rival offer sheets only land on **qualified** RFAs, arrive in two
  waves inside the window, and run a live match clock (`matchDaysLeft`). An
  unanswered sheet resolves itself instead of sitting inert until the stage ends.
- **B4** — `termPriceMultiplier` / `termSecurityScore` in `contracts.ts`. Extra
  years past the ask are a concession the CLUB is buying, so they raise the AAV
  he needs to see (more for bought UFA years, more again past 30). Both the
  async queue and the live negotiation table (`offerValue`) price through it, and
  the offer builder shows the bill per term. Meeting the ask exactly is still
  exactly 1.0 — the calibration didn't move.

Everything above is additive on `CareerSnapshot` (`resignOffers`,
`resignPatience`, `qualifyingOffers`, `offseason.resignDay`) and round-trips
through save/load. **Not done:** a sit-down in the negotiation screen still ends
in a handshake the moment the agent says yes — by design (docs/DEPTH §1.4), the
async queue is the surface that models the clock.

## Recommended order

1. **A6 + A7** (Continue gates) — small, and they stop the GM sleepwalking past the two biggest decisions.
2. **F1** (retirement odds) — small, self-contained, and it currently breaks every dynasty.
3. **G1** (Roster view dropdown) — an inert control hiding whole column sets.
4. **C3 + C4** (milestone correctness) — narrow detector bugs.
5. **D1 + D2** (calls + voice default).
6. **E1 + E2** (draft board + scout coherence).
7. **A1–A5** — the trade epic. Per-club valuation (A2) is the keystone; A3 falls out of it.
8. **B1–B4** — the re-signing window epic.
9. **C1 + C2** — match-night sim view (largest presentation piece).
