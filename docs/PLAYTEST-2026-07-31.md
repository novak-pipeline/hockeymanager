# Playtest notes — 2026-07-31 (user, Pittsburgh save)

Second full regroup. The nav ship-blocker is fixed, so this playtest reached
screens the last one couldn't. The findings cluster hard around **two systems
that are shallow where the game most needs depth**: the trade market and the
re-signing window. Everything else is polish, correctness or presentation.

Grouped by system, ranked within each. `bar` ties to docs/EXCELLENCE.md.

---

## A. THE TRADE SYSTEM — the headline epic

The user's words: *"trades are way too easy to make… we need DEEP realism and
intricacy to our value and trading system."* Treat A1–A3 as one epic; A4–A6 are
bugs that fall out of the same area.

| # | Finding | Notes | Bar |
|---|---------|-------|-----|
| A1 | **Trade value shown as a raw number** (`22.9`, `29.7`, `60.7`) | Wanted: a bar/meter like the EA NHL games, or another non-numeric read. A bare decimal reads like a spreadsheet and implies false precision. | B7.3 |
| A2 | **Value is global, not per-club** | *"each team should value players differently depending on their views of the player / their needs."* A rebuilder and a contender must not price the same 33-year-old alike; a club thin at RD must pay more for RD. This is the real fix behind A3 — one true value is what makes trades feel mechanical. | B3.2 |
| A3 | **Trades are far too easy** | Concrete: a 22-year-old at 4★ current / 5★ potential offered straight up for Bryan Rust (28, $5.13M×3). No AI club makes that trade. Needs real reluctance: retained salary, term, age curves, positional scarcity, roster fit, and a GM who says no. | B3.2 |
| A4 | **Only NHL-roster players are tradeable** | AHL players, prospects and rights-held juniors can't be moved. Picks too (verify). Guts the asset market. | B3.2 |
| A5 | **Trading Erik Karlsson produced no news article** | A franchise defenceman changes teams in silence. Should be a headline, a feed reaction, and a chronicle entry. | B4.5 |
| A6 | **Trade offers do not block Continue** | An open offer must gate the sim and be flagged in the topbar, the way other beats now name themselves (Gap #1). Right now you can sim straight past a standing offer. | B2.2 |
| A7 | **Can sim past the trade deadline** | The deadline is the season's biggest decision point and it slides by unattended. Must be a hard beat gate. | B3.2 |

## H. AUTOPILOT FINDINGS (same day, 3 seasons, imported 32-team, seed 2029)

The autonomous GM played three seasons the same day. It corroborates the user's
read and adds two crashes a human would only hit by luck.

**Corroboration worth noting:** 14 trades and 5 signings across three seasons.
The previous autopilot run reported **0 trades / 0 signings** — the Gap #8 market
fixes (league-only partner pool, adaptive shop floor) are working. The market is
alive; per the user it is now alive and *too easy*, which is exactly what A1–A3
addresses.

| # | Finding | Severity |
|---|---------|----------|
| H1 | **`acceptTrade` threw: "player p79 is not on Florida Panthers's roster."** A standing offer referenced a player who had since moved. Offers are not invalidated when their assets change hands — a stale offer is presented as live and crashes on accept. | critical |
| H2 | **`acceptTrade` threw: "Accepting would put you $3.8M over the cap."** The AI proposed a trade the club cannot legally accept, and the UI still offered Accept. Illegal offers must be filtered at generation, or the Accept disabled with the reason shown. | critical |
| H3 | **In-season cap violations persist** — $98.5M against a $92.0M ceiling on three separate days in 2026, and $101.1M vs $96.1M in 2027. Gap #4 closed the discretionary `callUp` path; something else still stacks salary uncapped. Find the remaining path. | major |

## Recommended order

1. **H1 + H2** (trade crashes) — two reproducible throws; a crash outranks everything.
2. **A6 + A7** (Continue gates) — small, and they stop the GM sleepwalking past the two biggest decisions.
3. **F1** (retirement odds) — small, self-contained, and it currently breaks every dynasty.
4. **G1** (Roster view dropdown) — an inert control hiding whole column sets.
5. **C3 + C4** (milestone correctness) — narrow detector bugs.
6. **D1 + D2** (calls + voice default).
7. **E1 + E2** (draft board + scout coherence).
8. **A1–A5** — the trade epic. Per-club valuation (A2) is the keystone; A3 falls out of it.
9. **B1–B4** — the re-signing window epic.
10. **C1 + C2** — match-night sim view (largest presentation piece).
