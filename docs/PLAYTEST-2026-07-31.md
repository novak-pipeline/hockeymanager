# Playtest notes — 2026-07-31 (user, Pittsburgh save)

Second full regroup. The nav ship-blocker is fixed, so this playtest reached
screens the last one couldn't. The findings cluster hard around **two systems
that are shallow where the game most needs depth**: the trade market and the
re-signing window. Everything else is polish, correctness or presentation.

Grouped by system, ranked within each. `bar` ties to docs/EXCELLENCE.md.

---

## A. THE TRADE SYSTEM — the headline epic

The user's words: *"trades are way too easy to make… we need DEEP realism and
intricacy to our value and trading system."* Treat A1–A3 as one epic; A4–A6 are
bugs that fall out of the same area.

| # | Finding | Notes | Bar |
|---|---------|-------|-----|
| A1 | **Trade value shown as a raw number** (`22.9`, `29.7`, `60.7`) | Wanted: a bar/meter like the EA NHL games, or another non-numeric read. A bare decimal reads like a spreadsheet and implies false precision. | B7.3 |
| A2 | **Value is global, not per-club** | *"each team should value players differently depending on their views of the player / their needs."* A rebuilder and a contender must not price the same 33-year-old alike; a club thin at RD must pay more for RD. This is the real fix behind A3 — one true value is what makes trades feel mechanical. | B3.2 |
| A3 | **Trades are far too easy** | Concrete: a 22-year-old at 4★ current / 5★ potential offered straight up for Bryan Rust (28, $5.13M×3). No AI club makes that trade. Needs real reluctance: retained salary, term, age curves, positional scarcity, roster fit, and a GM who says no. | B3.2 |
| A4 | ~~**Only NHL-roster players are tradeable**~~ **FIXED** | The trade builder now lists the whole organisation — NHL roster, AHL affiliate and rights-held juniors, grouped under "Farm & prospects" — and farm assets move org-to-org (no NHL roster slot, no cap hit) on both the propose and accept paths. Picks were already movable; now covered by a test. | B3.2 |
| A5 | ~~**Trading Erik Karlsson produced no news article**~~ **FIXED** | One `announceTrade()` path now covers every user trade, with the noise scaled to the best asset: franchise → BLOCKBUSTER headline + a guaranteed same-day insider break on the Feed + chronicle; notable → a named headline; depth → one quiet line. The headline is filed against YOUR club (it was filed against the partner's, so inbox curation didn't read it as your business). | B4.5 |
| A6 | **Trade offers do not block Continue** | An open offer must gate the sim and be flagged in the topbar, the way other beats now name themselves (Gap #1). Right now you can sim straight past a standing offer. | B2.2 |
| A7 | **Can sim past the trade deadline** | The deadline is the season's biggest decision point and it slides by unattended. Must be a hard beat gate. | B3.2 |

## Recommended order

1. **A6 + A7** (Continue gates) — small, and they stop the GM sleepwalking past the two biggest decisions.
2. **F1** (retirement odds) — small, self-contained, and it currently breaks every dynasty.
3. **G1** (Roster view dropdown) — an inert control hiding whole column sets.
4. **C3 + C4** (milestone correctness) — narrow detector bugs.
5. **D1 + D2** (calls + voice default).
6. **E1 + E2** (draft board + scout coherence).
7. **A1–A5** — the trade epic. Per-club valuation (A2) is the keystone; A3 falls out of it.
8. **B1–B4** — the re-signing window epic.
9. **C1 + C2** — match-night sim view (largest presentation piece).
